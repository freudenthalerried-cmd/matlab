## CrazyGames (Englisch)

**Short description (1 Satz)**
Build your realm, train a hero and defend the castle against goblins, knights, trolls and dragons in a colorful 3D tower defense.

**Description**
Burgwacht is a cozy 3D tower defense with a strategy twist. Build archer towers, cannons, frost mages and tesla coils along the road, but don't forget your economy: sawmills earn more next to forests, gold mines next to rocks, and glowing crystal veins power your strongest spells.

- 5 handcrafted maps: green valleys, autumn woods, desert pass, river valley and a volcanic ash mountain
- Endless mode for high scores
- A hero you can move around the battlefield, with level-ups and a whirlwind attack
- Barracks with soldiers that block the road – set your own rally point
- Castle upgrades unlock new towers and a castle ballista
- Boss waves with trolls and fire-breathing dragons
- Meteor and blizzard spells
- Collect gems, buy permanent upgrades, claim a daily bonus and unlock 11 achievements
- Plays great on desktop and mobile, in English and German

**Controls**
Mouse/touch: tap a building card, then tap a tile to build. Tap buildings to upgrade them. Drag to move the map, scroll or pinch to zoom.
Keyboard: 1–8 build · Space start wave · Q meteor · E blizzard · H hero · R whirlwind · P pause · F speed
